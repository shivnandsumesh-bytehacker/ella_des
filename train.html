⚠️ AWS SYSTEM GUIDE

What is AWS (Automatic Warning System)?
It is an automated track safety system used across the British rail network.

  • Green Signal: You will hear a clear bell chime. No action needed.
  • Yellow / Caution Signal: A warning buzzer rings and the sunflower dial turns black and yellow.
  • Your Action: Press [Q] on keyboard or tap the AWS Reset button within 2.5 seconds to acknowledge.
  • Failure: If not acknowledged, the train's emergency brakes apply automatically until a full stop.
